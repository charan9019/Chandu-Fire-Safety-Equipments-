What We Do

Complete Fire Safety
Services

We go beyond selling — from installation to training, we are with you at every step of your safety journey.

01
Extinguisher Service & AMC
Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts covering refilling, pressure testing, hydro testing, and certification. We handle all paperwork and scheduling.
  • Refilling & recharging all types
  • Hydrostatic pressure testing
  • CO₂ & DCP cartridge replacement
  • Annual certification
  • Record keeping & compliance
02
Hydrant & Pump Installation
End-to-end design, supply, installation, testing & commissioning of fire hydrant systems for factories, commercial buildings, hospitals & warehouses.
  • System design & layout planning
  • Pump set installation & wiring
  • Hose reel cabinet setup
  • Pressure testing & commissioning
  • Quarterly AMC servicing
03
Fire Alarm System Installation
Complete design and installation of addressable & conventional fire alarm systems. Control panel programming, detector placement and full site testing.
  • Site survey & zoning plan
  • Detector & call point installation
  • Control panel programming
  • Integration with building systems
  • Annual testing & maintenance
04
Corporate Safety Training
Hands-on fire safety training for employees across all industries. Theory + practical sessions with real equipment. Certificates issued after training.
  • Basic fire fighting techniques
  • Extinguisher operation drill
  • Emergency evacuation planning
  • First aid & fire marshal training
  • Mock drill & assessment
05
Fire Safety Audit & Compliance
Comprehensive fire safety audits for factories, offices, hospitals and housing complexes. Compliance reports as per NBC, NFPA & local fire department norms.
  • Full site risk assessment
  • NOC documentation support
  • Deficiency identification & rectification
  • Fire safety certificate assistance
06
Suppression System Design
Design and install automatic suppression systems — wet pipe sprinklers, dry pipe systems, FM-200 clean agent systems for data centers and server rooms.
  • Hydraulic calculation & design
  • Sprinkler head selection
  • FM-200 / Novec system supply
  • Wet & dry pipe systems
